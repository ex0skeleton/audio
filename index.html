Using Audio Wiz

Connect and listen

Open Audio Wiz on both devices.

On the device you want to listen on, choose Receive audio, then Create receiver code. Copy the code and send it to your other device.

On the other device, choose Send audio and select what you want to share. Paste the receiver code, then tap Start source & create reply. Allow access when asked.

Copy the reply back to the listening device. Paste it into the reply box, then tap Connect audio. If you cannot hear anything, tap Enable listening.

Choose what to share

Microphone: share your voice or sounds around you.

Audio file: choose a file, connect your devices, then press Play.

YouTube / browser tab audio: select the tab playing the video in the sharing window and turn on Share tab audio.

PC apps / entire system audio: choose Entire Screen and enable system audio if it is available. Use this for apps such as VLC or PowerPoint.

Adjust your sound

Use the listening-volume slider and your device’s media-volume buttons. Leave playback on Standard for normal use. Try Boost only if the sound is too quiet.

Use headphones to avoid echo. For clearer music, share the tab or PC audio directly instead of using a microphone.

Stop or change devices

Tap Disconnect to stop. To share a different source or start again, disconnect on both devices and create new codes. Keep the listening device awake with Audio Wiz open.

Need help?

If there is no sound, check that your source is playing, the sharing window has audio enabled, and both devices have their media volume turned up. Tap Enable listening if needed.

If a problem continues, export a log from each device while it happens and share them for troubleshooting.
