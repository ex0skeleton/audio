Using Audio Wiz

Connect your devices

Open the same HTTPS app on both devices. On the device that will play audio, select Receive audio and Create receiver code. Copy the full code to your sending device.

On the sending device select Send audio, choose a source, paste the receiver code, and tap Start source & create reply. Allow microphone or sharing access. Copy the generated reply back to the receiver, then tap Connect audio. Tap Enable listening if needed.

YouTube and browser audio

On your PC choose YouTube / browser tab audio. In Chrome or Edge’s sharing window choose the actual YouTube tab and turn on Share tab audio. Selecting Audio Wiz’s own tab will not capture YouTube. Start the YouTube video and check its player volume.

VLC, PowerPoint and other apps

Choose PC apps / entire system audio, select Entire Screen in the browser picker, and enable system audio if offered. Sharing an app window may not include audio. What is offered depends on your browser and operating system. To change sources after pairing, Disconnect on both devices and pair again.

Clearer, louder audio

Use direct tab/system audio for music rather than a microphone near PC speakers. Digital capture avoids room noise. Opus is negotiated by the browsers, with a sender bitrate ceiling of 192 kbps where supported; source and network quality still matter. Voice cleanup applies only to microphone input, and automatic microphone gain is disabled to avoid raising background hiss. Turn cleanup off when using a microphone for music.

Raise the original player’s volume and your phone’s media volume first. Standard playback uses the browser’s audio player and a 0–100% slider. Optional Boost playback supports up to 300%, with a limiter. Return to Standard if Boost is silent. It cannot remove static already captured. Use headphones to prevent feedback. Recordings contain the received stream before listening-volume boost.

How it works

The copied codes contain connection details, not audio. WebRTC sends audio between your browsers. GitHub Pages serves the app; there is no pairing server or app account. Some networks need an optional TURN relay. This is browser playback/recording, not a Windows microphone driver. Keep Android unlocked and the page open.

Connection and troubleshooting

Connected flashes orange once, then stays orange. Disconnect stops local capture/listening and clears the session. The other device may detect the disconnect after a short delay. Both devices need new codes to reconnect.

If silent, check the selected capture source, sharing audio checkbox, source/player volume, phone media volume and Enable listening. Check the source activity meter on the sender. For crackling or static, try direct YouTube-tab capture at 100% volume and compare against microphone capture. If it continues, export logs from both devices after reproducing it.
